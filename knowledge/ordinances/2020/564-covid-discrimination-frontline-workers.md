# MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO. 07-2020

AN ORDINANCE PROHIBITING ANY FORM OF DISCRIMINATION TOWARDS MEDICAL AND NON-MEDICAL FRONTLINE WORKERS, AND PATIENTS
WITH CONFIRMED, SUSPECTED AND PROBABLE CASES OF COVID-19 IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF CARMONA, PROVIDING PENALTIES FOR
VIOLATION THEREOF, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

*Introduced by: Hon. Cesar M. Loyola and Hon. Alberto H. Ocampo*
*Committee on Health, Energy and Environmental Protection*

## EXPLANATORY NOTE

WHEREAS, the Local Government Code of 1991 accords every local government unit (LGU) the power and authority to
promote the general welfare within its jurisdiction, including the promotion of health and safety of its
constituents, maintenance of peace and order, and the preservation of comfort and convenience of its inhabitants;

WHEREAS, since the rise of the Coronavirus Disease 2019 (COVID-19) cases in the Philippines, infected patients
including those suspected and the frontline workers are presently experiencing discrimination because of the
perceived danger they posed in spreading the virus;

WHEREAS, following reports of discrimination against frontline workers, the Department of Interior and Local
Government (DILG) is urging all LGUs nationwide to pass and enforce anti-discrimination and anti-harassment ordinances
to protect frontline workers fighting against the COVID-19 pandemic;

<!-- page 2 -->

WHEREAS, there is a need to protect and safeguard the rights of COVID-19 patients who are already experiencing severe
anxiety and our frontline workers who are rendering services and sacrifices in the fight against the COVID-19
pandemic;

*NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ENACTED BY THE SANGGUNIANG BAYAN IN REGULAR SESSION THAT:*

## SECTION 1. Short Title.

This Ordinance shall be known and cited as the "COVID-19 Anti-Discrimination Ordinance of 2020".

## SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy.

It is hereby declared as a policy of the Municipal Government to exercise its powers to take necessary and
appropriate actions for an efficient governance to promote the general welfare, and to ensure public health and
safety. By this end, the Municipal Government fully supports the National Government in all its efforts to institute
intensive, effective and efficient management and control of the COVID-19 situation.

## SECTION 3. Coverage.

The provisions of this Ordinance shall cover all persons within the territorial jurisdiction of the Municipality of
Carmona, Cavite.

## SECTION 4. Prohibited Acts.

The following acts shall be unlawful:

- (a) To commit any act or utterance which causes stigma, disgrace, shame, humiliation, harassment or otherwise
  discriminating against persons with confirmed, suspected or probable cases of COVID-19 including public and private
  doctors, nurses, health workers, emergency personnel and volunteers, service workers who are assigned to hospitals
  or other centers where these persons are being treated;
- (b) To commit any act or utterance which causes stigma, disgrace, humiliation, harassment, or otherwise
  discriminating against frontline workers including health professionals, police, military, essential services
  personnel, and government and non-government social workers;
- (c) To refuse assistance or deny service to persons with confirmed, suspected and probable cases of COVID-19, and to
  our frontline workers; and
- (d) To spread rumors, or utter information or any communication that causes unnecessary fear, anger, panic or
  violation of rights of persons above-mentioned.

<!-- page 3 -->

## SECTION 5. Penalties.

Any person who violates any of the provisions of this Ordinance shall be meted out the following penalties:

First (1st) Offense - A fine of One Thousand Pesos (PhP1,000.00) or an imprisonment of not more than one (1) month,
or both, at the discretion of a competent court;

Second (2nd) Offense - A fine of Two Thousand Pesos (PhP2,000.00) or an imprisonment of not more than two (2) months,
or both, at the discretion of a competent court;

Third (3rd) and Succeeding Offense - A fine of Two Thousand Five Hundred Pesos (PhP2,500.00) or an imprisonment of not
more than three (3) months, or both, at the discretion of a competent court;

If the offender is a public official or public employee, the maximum penalty shall be imposed.

## SECTION 6. Implementing Mechanisms.

The Carmona Municipal Police Station (CMPS) and barangay officials/*tanods*, in close coordination with the Carmona
Inter-Agency Task Force on COVID-19, are hereby tasked to apprehend violators of this Ordinance, keep a separate
record for the proper monitoring of the violations, and ensure effective and efficient enforcement of the provisions
of this Ordinance. Upon its approval, the Municipal Mayor, if deemed necessary, may issue supplemental guidelines and
implementing rules and regulations to ensure the proper implementation of this Ordinance.

## SECTION 7. Information Dissemination.

The Municipal Information, Tourism, Culture and Arts Office is hereby tasked to immediately inform the public upon
effectivity of this Ordinance.

## SECTION 8. Separability Clause.

If any provision or portion of this ordinance found to be a violation of the constitution and declared invalid, the
provisions or portion hereof which are not so declared shall remain to be in full force and effect.

## SECTION 9. Repealing Clause.

All ordinances, executive orders and rules and regulations or parts thereof, which are inconsistent or in conflict
with the provisions of this ordinance are hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

## SECTION 10. Effectivity Clause.

This Ordinance shall immediately take effect upon compliance of the mandatory posting requirement prescribed under RA
7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991.
