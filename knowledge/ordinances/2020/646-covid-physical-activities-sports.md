# MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO. 18-2020

AN ORDINANCE PRESCRIBING GUIDELINES ON THE CONDUCT OF HEALTH-ENHANCING PHYSICAL ACTIVITIES AND SPORTS DURING THE
CORONAVIRUS DISEASE 2019 (COVID-19) PANDEMIC

*Introduced by: Hon. Cesar M. Loyola and Hon. Tom Carlo M. Ardemer*
*Joint Committees on Health, Energy, & Environmental Protection and Youth & Sports Development*

## EXPLANATORY NOTE

WHEREAS, the Local Government Code of 1991 accords every local government unit (LGU) the power and authority to
promote the general welfare within its territorial jurisdiction, including the promotion of health and safety,
maintenance of peace and order, preservation of comfort and convenience of its inhabitants, among other things;

WHEREAS, the Philippine Sports Commission (PSC), Games and Amusement Board (GAB), and the Department of Health (DOH)
have issued Joint Administrative Order No. 2020-0001 dated July 27, 2020 prescribing guidelines on the conduct of
health-enhancing physical activities and sports during the COVID-19 pandemic;

WHEREAS, the continuous physical inactivity of many of our constituents due to prolonged community quarantine is
considered a risk factor on their physical and mental conditions;

WHEREAS, it is imperative that health-enhancing physical activities- and sports be integrated in people's normative
routine throughout the duration of the COVID-19 response in order to deliver the number one 5-point strategy of the
DOH which is "Increasing Resilience";

<!-- page 2 -->

WHEREAS, in light of the foregoing, there is a need for a legislative action to ensure compliance with the national
government guidelines and directives relative to the management and control of the COVID-19 risk;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE SANGGUNIANG BAYAN IN REGULAR SESSION ASSEMBLED THAT:

## Section 1. Short Title.

This Ordinance shall be known and cited as the "COVID-19 PHYSICAL AND SPORTS ACTIVITIES ORDINANCE" of the
Municipality of Carmona, Cavite".

## Section 2. Declaration of Policy.

It is hereby declared as a policy of the Municipal Government in exercising its powers to take necessary and
appropriate actions for an efficient governance to promote the general welfare, ensure public health and safety, and
maintain peace and order within the locality. By this end, the Municipal Government fully supports the National
Government in all its efforts to institute intensive, effective and efficient management and control of the COVID-19
situation.

## Section 3. Coverage.

This Ordinance covers the conduct of exercise, sports, and other forms of health-enhancing physical activities as well
as play-for-pay physical sports activities for the duration of the COVID-19 response within the territorial
jurisdiction of the Municipality of Carmona.

## Section 4. Guidelines on the Conduct of Physical and Sports Activities.

The following guidelines on the conduct of health-enhancing physical activities (HPAs) and sports shall be strictly
observed:

- a. Recreational and non-professional contact sports, activities, and events such as basketball, volleyball,
  football, baseball, tournaments, competitive events, athletic meets, and the likes shall be temporarily suspended
  regardless of risk severity, until an effective vaccine and/or appropriate standard of care for COVID-19 cases is
  developed.
- b. Professional contact sports and activities for Filipino professional players with scheduled tournaments abroad
  and professional sporting events may be allowed in low-risk areas, provided such events are compliant to the minimum
  public health standards. Provided further, that no live audience shall be permitted regardless of the risk severity,
  until an effective vaccine and/or appropriate standard of care for COVID-19 cases is developed.

<!-- page 3 -->

- c. Intrazonal movement within moderate risk or low risk areas, for the purpose of conducting HPAs and sports shall
  be categorized as essential travel. On the other hand, interzonal movement for the same purpose may be permitted,
  subject to compliance with applicable laws, guidelines, and protocols.
- d. Relevant entities shall administer appropriate engineering and administrative controls as provided for by DOH AO
  No. 2020-0015 entitled "*Guidelines on the Risk-Based Public Health Standards for COVID-19 Mitigation*" and its
  iterations, such as but not limited to monitoring of symptoms, placement of handwashing facilities and sanitizers,
  and routine disinfection of frequently touched surfaces and equipment for areas or settings or facilities that will
  be used for physical activities.
- e. All individuals however are highly encouraged to carry out physical activities and sports within their areas of
  domicile.
- f. In High Risk Areas - The conduct of physical activities and sports in areas placed under:
  - i. Enhanced Community Quarantine (ECQ) -
    - limited to solo or individual activities; and
    - conducted indoors or in private space;
  - ii. Modified Enhanced Community Quarantine (MECQ) -
    - limited to individual sports or physical activities such as outdoor walk, jogging/running, biking, etc.; and
    - limited to outdoor exercises or physical activities;
- g. In Moderate Risk Areas - The following guidelines on the conduct of physical activities and sports shall be
  observed in these areas under:
  - i. General Community Quarantine (GCQ) -
    - indoor or outdoor non-contact and non-professional sports or activities and strength and sport-specific
      training;
    - individual or non-contact dual sports such as cycling, badminton, golf, *sipa*, *arnis*, etc.;
    - General Fitness Aerobic and Anaerobic Exercises such as running, cycling sprints, zumba, etc.; and
    - maximum of five (5) per sport/activity.
  - ii. Modified General Community Quarantine (MGCQ) with modifiers -
    - limited to indoor and outdoor HPAs and sports for small groups of not more than 10 participants.

<!-- page 4 -->

      However, contact skills training and other activities with deliberate body contact drills such as wrestling,
      holding, tackling or binding, etc. shall not be allowed;
    - use and sharing of sporting equipment (e.g. football, tennis, balls, weights, and mats) and outdoor exercise
      equipment in parks and other public spaces may be permitted;
    - communal and shared spaces or facilities for physical activities such as swimming pools, commercial gyms,
      bootcamps, yoga, pilates, dance, zumba, and spinning classes may be permitted to operate provided that the
      minimum public health standards are met.
- h. In Low Risk Areas (MGCQ) - The conduct of physical activities and sports in these areas under MGCQ shall permit:
  - i. indoor or outdoor non-contact sports or activities for non-professional sports and contact sports or
    activities for professional sports for a maximum of 10 and 20 participants per non-professional and professional
    sport/activity, respectively;
  - ii. non-contact skills training is allowed. Training and other activities with deliberate body contact drills such
    as wrestling, holding, tackling or binding, etc. shall not be allowed;
  - iii. use and sharing of sporting equipment (e.g. football, tennis, balls, weights, and mats) and outdoor exercise
    equipment in parks and other public spaces may be permitted; and
  - iv. communal and shared spaces or facilities for physical activities such as swimming pools, gyms, yoga studios,
    dance and fitness studios are permitted to operate subject to the minimum public health standards and the
    limitation on the maximum number of persons allowed to be involved in physical activities;
- i. Individuals shall practice at least two-meter physical distancing during the physical activity, proper
  handwashing, and respiratory etiquette.
- j. Commercial physical activity-related facilities shall ensure proper ventilation (e.g. open windows, installation
  of high-efficiency particulate air filtration systems or HEPA), and observance of the maximum number of participants
  allowed at a given time. Provided further, that such facilities comply with the guidelines issued by the Department
  of Trade and Industry (DTI).

<!-- page 5 -->

- k. Sharing of non-personal equipment is permitted, provided these are routinely disinfected before and after every
  use. However, equipment that is used orally or that has prolonged contact with the face shall not be shared or
  rented out.
- l. All concerned entities/individuals shall routinely carry out disinfection and sanitation procedures on frequently
  touched surfaces at home and in open areas and shared equipment. Ensure the installation of footbaths, handwashing
  facilities and dispensers with alcohol-based solutions.
- m. All concerned entities/individuals shall ensure compliance with policies and protocols for temperature and
  symptoms monitoring.
- n. All concerned entities/individuals shall cause the placement of marks and signs for the observance of physical
  distancing and other preventive measures.
- o. Professional players and individuals who are engaging in high-intensity exercise or workouts may be allowed not
  to wear masks DURING the conduct of exercise or physical activity ONLY, subject to the observance of at least two (2)
  meters physical distancing in outdoor facilities or three (3) meters in enclosed and indoor facilities and other
  preventive measures (e.g. placement of barriers between individuals, proper ventilation, etc.). Individuals engaging
  in physical activities in public spaces or high-density areas are recommended to wear their masks, but maintain the
  conduct of physical activity to a low or moderate intensity. If unable to wear a mask due to high-intensity
  exercises, face shields may be worn to prevent COVID-19 transmission.
- p. In line with the Omnibus Guidelines on the Implementation of Community Quarantine in the Philippines, the
  promoter of the sports event is discouraged from allowing athletes, coaches, and other personnel who fall under any
  of the following categories to resume training and engage in other physical activities:
  - i. Individuals below twenty-one (21) years old;
  - ii. Individuals who are sixty (60) years old and above;
  - iii. Individuals of any age with co-morbidities or pre-existing illness; and
  - iv. Women with high-risk pregnancy.

  The promoter shall provide alternative arrangements to such players and personnel with no diminution in fees or
  salaries.
- q. All sports or physical fitness facilities shall designate a Health and Safety Officer to perform the following
  functions:

<!-- page 6 -->

  - i. Ensure compliance to the minimum public health standards during the conduct of allowed physical activities and
    establish mechanisms to monitor compliance with the said standards;
  - ii. Monitor the health status and symptoms of athletes and personnel;
  - iii. Use the Carmona COVID-19 Tracker (Carmona Digital Contact Tracer) App or keep records, through minimal
    contact means, of individuals who used the facilities for the purpose of contact tracing in the event the need for
    such arises; and
  - iv. Refer symptomatic individuals to appropriate health authority/health facilities, as necessary.
- r. All players, coaches/managers, promoter, and other personnel exhibiting flu-like symptoms including but not
  limited to fever, dry cough, and tiredness shall be reported immediately to the Barangay Health Emergency Response
  Team (BHERT), Municipal Health Office, and/or other appropriate authorities/facilities for monitoring and
  observance of the following protocols:
  - i. All symptomatic individuals shall be isolated immediately;
  - ii. The promoter, through the Health and Safety Officer, shall compile records of all individuals who have been
    on-site or who have had close contact with the suspect case/s;
  - iii. If the suspect case is confirmed COVID-19 positive, the promoter shall provide the records to the Municipal
    Epidemiology and Surveillance Unit (MESU) to facilitate contact tracing;
  - iv. All close contacts shall be notified and undergo a 14-day self-quarantine with specific instructions from the
    Health and Safety Officer and/or MHO on monitoring of symptoms and possible next steps;
  - v. All facilities, surfaces, and equipment used shall be rigorously disinfected; and
  - vi. In the event that an individual is confirmed COVID-19 positive, the conduct of training and other activities
    shall be suspended.

## Section 5. Penalties

Any person or entity in violation of any of the abovementioned guidelines shall be given citation ticket for every
offense and in addition thereof, be imposed with the following penalties:

A. FOR INDIVIDUALS:

- a. First (1st) offense - Any person caught in violation of any of the prohibited acts shall be meted a penalty of
  fine of ONE THOUSAND FIVE HUNDRED PESOS (PhP 1,500.00) and/or Community Service of at least THREE (3) days but not
  more than FIVE (5) days.
- b. Second (2nd) offense - Any violator caught for the second time shall be imposed a penalty of fine of TWO
  THOUSAND PESOS (PhP 2,000.00) and/or Community service of not less than FIVE (5) days but not to exceed TEN (10)
  days.
- c. Third (3rd) offense and oftener - Any violator caught for the third time or oftener, shall be meted a penalty
  of fine of TWO THOUSAND FIVE HUNDRED PESOS (PhP 2,500.00) and/or IMPRISONMENT OF NOT LESS THAN TWO (2) MONTHS BUT
  NOT MORE THAN SIX (6) MONTHS upon discretion of the court.

B. FOR ESTABLISHMENTS

- a. First (1st) offense - Any establishment found to have violated any of the prohibited acts shall be meted a fine
  of ONE THOUSAND FIVE HUNDRED PESOS (PhP 1,500.00) and SUSPENSION OF BUSINESS PERMIT FOR ONE (1) MONTH.
- b. Second (2nd) offense - Any establishment found to have violated any of the prohibited acts for the second time
  shall be meted a fine of TWO THOUSAND PESOS (PhP 2,000.00) and SUSPENSION OF BUSINESS PERMIT FOR TWO (2) MONTHS.
- c. Third (3rd) offense - Any establishment found to have violated any of the prohibited acts for the third time
  shall be meted a fine of TWO THOUSAND FIVE HUNDRED PESOS (PhP 2,500.00) AND REVOCATION OF BUSINESS PERMIT WITH
  CLOSURE.

  In the case of corporations, partnerships or associations, the president, general manager, or person-in-charge of
  such establishment shall be held liable therefor.

<!-- page 8 -->

C. FOR MINORS AND INDIVIDUALS ABOVE 60 YEARS OF AGE

- a. First (1st) offense - If the minor was found to have violated any of the prohibited acts, the minor shall be
  referred to the barangay hall where the minor resides, or if the minor is not a resident, to the nearest barangay
  hall to be handled by the Barangay Council for the Protection of Children (BCPC) as focal person. Thereafter, the
  parent/guardian shall be summoned and informed of the infraction and apprehension including the details thereof.

  The BCPC shall conduct a discussion or counseling with the minor together with the parent/guardian.

  Before turning over the minor, the parent/guardian shall be informed of the consequences they will be dealing with
  in case of subsequent violation of the minor pursuant to the applicable provisions under this Code.

  Further, the parent/guardian of the minor shall pay a penalty of fine amounting to ONE THOUSAND FIVE HUNDRED PESOS
  (PhP 1,500.00) and render TWENTY-FOUR (24) HOURS OF COMMUNITY SERVICE.
- b. Second (2nd) offense - If the minor was found to have violated any of the prohibited acts for the second time,
  the minor shall undergo the same procedure as provided for under the First (1st) offense.

  Further, the parent/guardian of the minor shall pay a penalty of TWO THOUSAND PESOS (PhP 2,000.00) and render
  THIRTY-SIX (36) HOURS OF COMMUNITY SERVICE.
- c. Third (3rd) offense and oftener - If the minor violated any of the prohibited acts for the third time and
  oftener, the minor shall, in addition to the conditions set forth in the preceding paragraphs, be turned over to the
  Municipal Social Welfare and Development (MSWD) Officer to undergo Intervention programs prescribed by Republic Act
  9344, otherwise known as the Juvenile Justice and Welfare Act of 2006.

  Further, the parent/guardian of the minor shall pay a penalty of fine amounting to TWO THOUSAND FIVE HUNDRED PESOS
  (PhP 2,500.00) and render FORTY-EIGHT (48) HOURS OF COMMUNITY SERVICE.

<!-- page 9 -->

  The minor and the parent/guardian shall submit a Certificate of Compliance duly issued by the MSWD Officer stating
  among others that the fine had been paid showing proof thereof and that the parent/guardian had already completed
  the number of hours of community service.

  If the offender is an individual of 60 years and above, the maximum penalty of fine prescribed herein shall be
  imposed in any stage of offense.

## Section 6. Implementation

The duly created Task Force is hereby directed to strictly enforce and implement this Code. To ensure the effective
enforcement of this Code, the Carmona IATF may issue supplemental guidelines and rules and regulations, subject to the
approval of the Municipal Mayor.

## Section 7. Information Dissemination

The Municipal Information, Tourism, Culture and Arts Office (MITCAO) is hereby tasked to immediately inform the
public upon effectivity of this ordinance.

## Section 8. *Repealing Clause.*

All municipal ordinances, Implementing Rules and Regulations, and Executive Orders, or parts thereof, which are
inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed and/or modified accordingly.

## Section 9. Separability Clause

If any provision or portion of this ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid by a competent court, the
provisions or portions hereof which are not so declared shall remain in full force and effect.

## Section 10. Supplementary Clause.

On matters not provided for in this Ordinance, any existing applicable laws and their corresponding Implementing
Rules and Regulations, Executive Orders and relevant issuances issued therefor particularly PSC, GAB, and DOH JAO No.
2020-0001 and its Annexes A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, and C2 shall apply in a supplemental manner.

## Section 11. Effectivity Clause

This Ordinance shall take effect upon compliance with the mandatory posting and/or publication requirement
prescribed under the Local Government Code of 1991, and shall remain in full force and effect during the COVID-19
pandemic.

ENACTED ON 21 SEPTEMBER 2020.
