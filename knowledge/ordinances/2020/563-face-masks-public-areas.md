# MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO. 06-2020

AN ORDINANCE MANDATING THE WEARING OF FACE MASKS OR OTHER PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT BY ALL PERSONS IN PUBLIC AREAS DURING
THE ENHANCED COMMUNITY QUARANTINE, PROVIDING PENALTIES FOR VIOLATION THEREOF, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

*Introduced by: Hon. Cesar M. Loyola and Hon. Bolivar I. Babila*
*Committee on Health, Energy and Environmental Protection*

## EXPLANATORY NOTE

WHEREAS, on 08 March 2020, by virtue of Proclamation No. 922 and in pursuance to Section 7 of RA 11332, His
Excellency, President Rodrigo Roa Duterte declared a State of Public Health Emergency throughout the Philippines upon
confirmation by the Department of Health (DOH) of the Coronavirus Disease 2019 (COVID-19) local transmission in the
Philippines;

WHEREAS, the President further issued Proclamation No. 929 on 16 March 2020, declaring a state of calamity throughout
the Philippines and imposing an enhanced community quarantine throughout Luzon beginning 17 March until 12 April 2020
due to COVID-19;

WHEREAS, the Local Government Code of 1991 accords every local government unit (LGU) the power and authority to
promote the general welfare within its jurisdiction, including the promotion of health and safety of its constituents;

<!-- page 2 -->

WHEREAS, the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG), through Memorandum Circular No. 2020-071 dated
09 April 2020, enjoins all LGUs to enact an ordinance providing for the mandatory wearing of face masks or other
protective equipment in public areas and for the imposition of appropriate sanctions thereof;

WHEREAS, as the number of confirmed cases of COVID-19 consistently surges, serious threat to the health, safety,
security, and lives of the constituents is alarming;

WHEREAS, the mandatory wearing of face masks or other protective equipment by all residents can effectively lessen the
transmission of COVID-19, whenever allowed to go out of their residences pursuant to existing guidelines issued by the
national government;

WHEREAS, notwithstanding serious efforts, stern warnings, constant pleas, and preventive measures being applied by the
Municipal Government to help the national government flatten the curve of the COVID-19 pandemic, some constituents are
showing defiance on the threat brought about by the contagious disease in the present situation;

WHEREAS, in light of the foregoing, there is need for a legislative action to make it mandatory for all persons to
wear face masks or other protective equipment to ensure strict compliance of the citizenry to the government guidelines
and directives relative to the management and control of the COVID-19 risk;

*NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE SANGGUNIANG BAYAN IN REGULAR SESSION THAT:*

## SECTION 1. Short Title.

This Ordinance shall be known and cited as the "Mandatory Wearing of Face Mask Due to COVID-19 Ordinance of Carmona,
Cavite".

## SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy.

It is hereby declared as a policy of the Municipal Government to exercise its powers to take necessary and appropriate
actions for an efficient governance to promote the general welfare, and to ensure public health and safety. By this
end, the Municipal Government fully supports the National Government in all its efforts to institute intensive,
effective and efficient management and control of the COVID-19 situation.

## SECTION 3. Coverage.

The provisions of this Ordinance shall cover all persons within the territorial jurisdiction of the Municipality of
Carmona, Cavite.

<!-- page 3 -->

## SECTION 4. Prohibition.

It shall be unlawful for any person to go out of her/his residence, or enter any government buildings or facilities,
workplaces, commercial/industrial establishments, or any public areas, without wearing a face mask or other protective
equipment during the enhanced community quarantine.

Under this Ordinance, a face mask is a protective mask used to cover the nose, mouth and chin with specifications and
proper use as recommended by the DOH (e.g. surgical masks, medical masks or N95 masks) or reusable or washable face
masks ordinarily sold in commercial establishments.

However, due to the prevailing shortage of supplies, wearing of improvised masks and other protective equipment, such
as indigenous, reusable, homemade or do-it-yourself (DIY) masks, face shields, and handkerchiefs, that can reduce risks
of local transmission of COVId-19, shall be allowed.

## SECTION 5. Business Establishments Compliance Requirements.

All operational business establishments, whether commercial or industrial, shall not allow entry of their employees or
any person without wearing a face mask or an equivalent thereof in said establishment. Further, said establishments
shall provide a face mask for their every employee and display the full copy of this Ordinance at or near the entrance
of their workplace.

## SECTION 6. Penalties.

Any person who violates any of the provisions of this Ordinance shall be meted out the following penalties:

First (1st) Offense - A fine of One Thousand Pesos (PhP1,000.00) or an imprisonment of not more than one (1) month, or
both, at the discretion of a competent court;

Second (2nd) Offense - A fine of Two Thousand Pesos (PhP2,000.00) or an imprisonment of not more than two (2) months,
or both, at the discretion of a competent court;

Third (3rd) and Succeeding Offenses - A fine of Two Thousand Five Hundred Pesos (PhP2,500.00) or an imprisonment of not
more than three (3) months, or both, at the discretion of a competent court;

<!-- page 4 -->

## SECTION 7. Implementing Mechanisms.

The Carmona Municipal Police Station (CMPS) and barangay officials/*tanods*, in close coordination with the Carmona
Inter-Agency Task Force on COVID-19, are hereby tasked to apprehend violators of this Ordinance, keep a separate record
for the proper monitoring of the violations, and ensure effective and efficient enforcement of the provisions of this
Ordinance. Upon its approval, the Municipal Mayor, if deemed necessary, may issue supplemental guidelines and
implementing rules and regulations to ensure the proper implementation of this Ordinance.

## SECTION 8. Information Dissemination.

The Municipal Information, Tourism, Culture and Arts Office is hereby tasked to immediately inform the public upon
effectivity of this Ordinance.

## SECTION 9. Effectivity.

This Ordinance shall immediately take effect upon compliance of the mandatory posting requirement prescribed under RA
7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991.

ENACTED ON 13 APRIL 2020.
