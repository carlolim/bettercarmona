# MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO. 09-2020

AN ORDINANCE MANDATING THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE "*BAYAN MAG-LOG SA CARMONA COVID-19 TRACKING SYSTEM* (BML-CCTS) OF
THE MUNICIPAL GOVERNMENT OF CARMONA FOR THE PURPOSES OF TRACKING AND PREVENTING THE SPREAD OF CORONAVIRUS DISEASE
(COVID-19) AND IMPOSING PENALTIES FOR VIOLATION THEREOF

Introduced by: Hon. Huberto A. Hugo and Hon. Cesar M. Loyola
Joint Committees on Information & Technology and Health & Environmental Protection

## EXPLANATORY NOTE

WHEREAS, the Local Government Code of 1991 accords every local government unit (LGU) the power and authority to
promote the general welfare within its jurisdiction, including the promotion of health and safety of its
constituents, maintenance of peace and order, among others;

WHEREAS, considering that the country is under the State of Public Health Emergency and the contact tracing of
COVID-19 patients is deemed necessary to prevent the spread of the virus;

WHEREAS, the Information Technology (IT) Unit of the Municipal Government of Carmona has developed a COVID-19
tracking system (BML-CCTS) via a mobile application that proactively logs vital information, interactions, and
self-checking on health conditions/symptoms of individuals within the municipality that could help track and prevent
COVID-19 contagion. This modern technology

<!-- page 2 -->

application enables proper authorities to automate their contact tracing efforts in a safe, easy, and timely manner.
The system will alert the users regarding the infected person's close contacts in the prior 14 days;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE SANGGUNIANG BAYAN IN REGULAR SESSION THAT:

## SECTION 1. Short Title.

This Ordinance shall be known and cited as the "Bayan Mag-Log sa Carmona COVID-19 Tracking System (BML-CCTS)
Ordinance".

## SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy.

It is hereby declared as a policy of the Municipal Government to exercise its powers to take necessary and appropriate
actions for efficient governance to promote the general welfare and ensure public health and safety. It is also the
policy of this Municipal Government to ensure that personal information in the BML-CCTS is secured and protected.
Municipal officials and employees shall ensure at all times the confidentiality of any personal information that has
been processed and shall be used only to track and prevent the spread of COVID-19 infections.

## SECTION 3. Coverage.

This Ordinance shall apply to all residents and non-Carmona residents who will enter, work or do business in the
Municipality of Carmona, Cavite.

## SECTION 4. Limited Personal Information.

Personal information of persons covered by this Ordinance shall be limited to full name, birth date, gender, address,
and contact number. Smartphone users shall be given a unique identification (ID) after signing up and shall be
required to log in once to the BML-CCTS when they go out of their residences and log every interaction made. However,
non-smartphone users shall be required to present their valid ID to PNP personnel/barangay tanods for the purpose of
logging in to the BML-CCTS. It is the responsibility of the IT Unit, in coordination with the Carmona Municipal Police
Station (CMPS) and the barangays, to collect and process the data every day.

## SECTION 5. Prohibited Acts.

The following acts are hereby declared unlawful during the COVID-19 pandemic:

- a. Going out of the residence, working or doing business in the municipality without logging in to the BML-CCTS,
  activating its application, and logging his/her interactions;
- b. Providing false information to the proper authorities about his/her identity, interactions and/or movements that
  may have posed significant risk to public health or resulted in the transmission or spread of COVID-19 infections;
  and
- c. Unauthorized disclosure of personal information in the BML-CCTS.

<!-- page 3 -->

## SECTION 6. Penalties.

(a) Any person who violates any of the provisions of this Ordinance shall be meted out the following penalties:

First (1st) Offense - A fine of One Thousand Five Hundred Pesos (PhP1,500.00) or an imprisonment of not more than one
(1) month, or both, at the discretion of a competent court;

Second (2nd) Offense - A fine of Two Thousand Pesos (PhP2,000.00) or an imprisonment of one (1) month but not more
than two (2) months, or both, at the discretion of a competent court; and

Third (3rd) and Succeeding Offenses - A fine of Two Thousand Five Hundred Pesos (PhP2,500.00) or an imprisonment of
two (2) months but not more than three (3) months, or both, at the discretion of a competent court;

(b) In case the offender is a government official or employee, the maximum penalty shall be imposed without prejudice
to the filing of administrative, civil and/or criminal action/s.

(c) In addition to the penalties imposed, he/she cannot go out of his/her residence, work or engage in business.

## SECTION 7. Implementing Mechanisms.

The CMPS and barangay officials/*tanods*, in close coordination with the Carmona Inter-Agency Task Force on COVID-19,
are hereby tasked to apprehend violators of this Ordinance, keep a separate record for the proper monitoring of the
violations, and ensure effective and efficient enforcement of the provisions of this Ordinance. The concerned CMPS
personnel shall file appropriate charge/s for the violation of this Ordinance.

## SECTION 8. Information Dissemination.

The Municipal Information, Tourism, Culture and Arts Office (MITCAO) is hereby tasked to immediately inform the public
upon effectivity of this Ordinance.

## SECTION 9. Separability Clause.

If any provision or portion of this ordinance is found to be a violation of the constitution and declared invalid,
the provisions or portion hereof which are not so declared shall remain to be in full force and effect.

## SECTION 10. Repealing Clause.

All ordinances, executive orders and rules and regulations or parts thereof, which are inconsistent or in conflict
with the provisions of this ordinance are hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

## SECTION 11. Effectivity Clause.

This Ordinance shall immediately take effect upon compliance of the mandatory posting requirement prescribed under
the Local Government Code of 1991.
