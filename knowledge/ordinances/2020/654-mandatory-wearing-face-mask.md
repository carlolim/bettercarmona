# MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO. 26-2020

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTIONS 8 AND 9, ARTICLE IV (MANDATORY WEARING OF FACE MASK) OF MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO.
15-2020, ENTITLED "AN ORDINANCE CODIFYING ENACTED ORDINANCES RELATIVE TO COVID-19, IMPOSING UNIFORM PENALTIES THEREOF,
AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES" (CARMONA COVID-19 CODE)

*Introduced by: Hon. Cesar M. Loyola and Hon. Alberto H. Ocampo*
*Committee on Health, Energy and Environmental Protection*

## EXPLANATORY NOTE

WHEREAS, the Local Government Code of 1991 accords every local government unit (LGU) the power and authority to
promote the general welfare within its jurisdiction, including the promotion of health and safety of its constituents;

WHEREAS, the Inter-Agency Task Force for the Management of Emerging Infectious Diseases (IATF) issued Resolution No.
88-2020 on 14 December 2020 requiring Filipinos to wear full-coverage face shields and face masks in all public places;

WHEREAS, to conform with IATF Resolution No. 88-2020, Municipal Ordinance No. 15-2020 needs to be amended particularly
Sections 8 and 9, Article IV thereof;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE SANGGUNIANG BAYAN IN REGULAR SESSION ASSEMBLED THAT:

<!-- page 2 -->

## SECTION 1.

Sections 8 and 9, Article IV of said Ordinance are hereby amended as follows:

"ARTICLE IV
MANDATORY WEARING OF FACE SHIELD AND FACE MASK

Section 8. Mandatory Wearing of Face Shield and Face Mask - It is hereby mandated the wearing of full-coverage face
shields together with face masks, earloop masks, indigenous, reusable, or do-it-yourself masks, or other facial
protective equipment which can effectively lessen the transmission of COVID-19, by all persons whenever they go out of
their residences.

Section 9. Business Establishments Compliance Requirements - All operational business establishments, whether
commercial or industrial, shall not allow entry of their employees, customers, or any person without wearing
full-coverage face shield and face mask or an equivalent thereof in said establishment. Further, said establishments
shall provide face shield and face mask for their employees and display the full copy of this Ordinance at or near the
entrance of their workplace."

## SECTION 2.

This Ordinance shall take effect upon compliance with the mandatory posting and/or publication requirement prescribed
under R.A. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991.

ENACTED ON 21 DECEMBER 2020.
