# ORDINANCE NO. 03-2024

AN ORDINANCE LOCALIZING REPUBLIC ACT NUMBER 11036, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE "MENTAL HEALTH ACT" THEREBY CREATING A
MENTAL HEALTH TASK FORCE, DEFINING ITS DUTIES AND FUNCTIONS, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

*Introduced by:* Hon. Bernie A. Ortiz and Hon. Rebecca L. Reyes
*SP Joint Committees on Education & Health and Social Services*

## EXPLANATORY NOTE

WHEREAS, Section 16 of the Local Government Code of 1991 [Republic Act (RA) No. 7160] accords every local government
unit power and authority to promote the general welfare within its territorial jurisdiction, including the mandate to
promote public health and safety, preserve the comfort and convenience of the inhabitants, among other things;

WHEREAS, RA No. 11036, otherwise known as the "*Mental Health Act*", establishes a National Mental Health Policy for
the purposes of enhancing the delivery of integrated mental health services, promoting and protecting the rights of
persons utilizing psychosocial health services, appropriating funds therefor, and for other purposes;

WHEREAS, Section 37 (a) of the said Act mandates local government units (LGUs) to review, formulate, and develop the
regulations and guidelines necessary to implement an effective mental health care and wellness policy within the
territorial jurisdiction of each LGU, including the passage of a local ordinance on the subject of mental health,
consistent with existing relevant national policies and guidelines;

WHEREAS, the City Government of Carmona commits itself to promoting the well-being of the constituents especially the
vulnerable sectors by ensuring that mental health is valued, promoted, and protected;

WHEREAS, it is for the best interest of this City to come up with whole-of-society and whole-of-government approaches
and measures by implementing existing policies, programs, strategies, and regulations relative to mental health;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE SANGUNIANG PANLUNGSOD IN REGULAR SESSION ASSEMBLED THAT:

<!-- page 2 -->

## Section 1.

This Ordinance shall be known and cited as the "Mental Health Ordinance" of the City of Carmona, Cavite.

## Section 2.

There is hereby created a Carmona Mental Health Task Force (CMHTF) which shall be composed of the following:

- Chairperson - City Health Officer
- Secretariat - Health Education and Promotion Officer
- Members:
  - SP Committee Chairperson on Health
  - Liga ng mga Barangay President
  - SK City Federation President
  - City Social Welfare and Development Officer
  - PBC Chief of Hospital
  - City Legal Officer
  - Community Affairs and Youth Development Office Head
  - Persons with Disability Affairs Officer
  - Human Resource Management Officer
  - Public Employment Service Office Manager
  - CDRRMO Head
  - City Local Government Operations Officer
  - CCPS Chief of Police
  - Schools District Supervisor
  - ALLSHS Principal/Assistant Principal
  - Carmona National High School Principal
  - Cavite State University - Carmona Campus Dean
  - Carmona PTA Federation President
  - Iskolar ng Bayan ng Carmona, Cavite
  - Student government organizations
  - Religious sector representative/s
  - Three (3) representatives from civil society organizations involved in mental health concerns

## Section 3.

The Task Force may include other agencies or stakeholders not mentioned in this Ordinance having an immense part in
its implementation and who shall be named as the expanded members of the Task Force.

The Task Force may likewise create different committees, designate members thereof, and define the roles for each
committee to ensure the implementation and sustainability of the Mental Health Program.

<!-- page 3 -->

## Section 4.

The presence of a majority of the members of the CMHTF shall constitute a quorum.

## Section 5.

The CMHTF shall meet at least once quarterly or as frequently as necessary to discharge its duties and functions. The
CMHTF shall be convened by the Chairperson or upon written request of at least three (3) of its members.

## Section 6.

The CMHTF shall perform the following duties and functions:

- a. Formulate, adopt, and implement policies, programs, strategies, activities, and the likes in furtherance of the
  applicable provisions of the Mental Health Act and its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR);
- b. Formulate, develop, implement, and sustain a city mental health program. In coordination with relevant national
  government agencies, barangays, and concerned stakeholders, create a framework for Mental Health Awareness Program to
  promote effective strategies regarding mental healthcare, its components, and services, as well as to improve
  awareness on stigmatized medical conditions;
- c. Adopt an integrated and comprehensive approach to the development of the Community-based Mental Health Care
  Delivery System and facility, if applicable, to deliver appropriate services and interventions including provision of
  mental health protection, care, treatment, and other essential services to those with mental illness or disability;
- d. Provide consistent, rational, unified, pro-active, and effective response and solution to mental health problems
  and concerns;
- e. Tap mental health professionals and experts to bolster government actions and efforts in combating mental health
  problems and issues within the locality;
- f. Network with government, non-government, and private institutions and other stakeholders for the identification,
  management, and prevention of mental health conditions. Likewise, for the establishment of relevant research and
  development programs on mental health;
- g. Formulate, adopt, and implement policies and programs for the promotion of mental health as well as in addressing
  discrimination or stigmatization issues in educational institutions and workplaces within the City;
- h. Establish and facilitate referral mechanisms of individuals with mental health conditions to treatment and
  psychosocial support;
- i. Conduct capacity-building, orientation, seminar, and/or training of local health workers, schools' guidance
  counselors, teachers, parents, and different sectors of the community through the help of mental health
  professionals and experts;

<!-- page 4 -->

- j. Establish and maintain helplines, official social media accounts, and physical help desks for mental health
  concerns;
- k. Initiate and sustain a city-wide multimedia campaign to raise the level of awareness on the promotion and
  protection of mental health and rights including, but not limited to, mental health and nutrition, stress handling,
  guidance and counseling, and other issues and concerns relative thereto; and
- l. Perform such other duties and functions as may be deemed necessary by the CMHTF, subject to the approval of the
  City Mayor.

## Section 7.

All patients or service users with mental illness shall enjoy the right to confidentiality. All information,
communications, and records, in whatever form or medium stored, regarding the patient/service user, or any treatment or
care thereof, shall not be disclosed to third party without the written consent of the patient/service user concerned,
except in some circumstances allowed by law. Violation of this Section shall be penalized in accordance with the
existing laws, ordinances, and/or issuances.

## Section 8.

The month of October is hereby declared as the Mental Health Month in the City of Carmona. All establishments, offices,
institutions, civil society organizations, and the people of Carmona are hereby enjoined to observe this important
event, and to engage in other forms of relevant activities that will promote mental health awareness under the
auspices of the CMHTF and CHO in coordination with the concerned national government agencies, barangays, and other
stakeholders.

## Section 9.

All barangay governments shall enact their respective ordinances localizing and implementing the Mental Health
Programs of the National and Local Governments including the pertinent provisions of the Mental Health Act and its IRR.

## Section 10.

A funding support amounting to PhP200,000.00 for the purposes stated hereunder is hereby appropriated which shall be
taken from the Annual Local Expenditure Program FY 2024, subject to the usual accounting and auditing procedures. Such
fund as well as the annual appropriation thereof shall be exclusively used to defray necessary or incidental expenses
to be incurred for purposes of implementing the applicable provisions of Mental Health Act, existing rules and
regulations, and this Ordinance.

## Section 11.

If, for any reason or reasons, any part or provision of this Ordinance is held unconstitutional or invalid, other parts
or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

<!-- page 5 -->

## Section 12.

All ordinances, implementing rules and regulations, and executive orders, or parts thereof, which are inconsistent with
the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed and/or modified accordingly.

## Section 13.

On matters not provided for in this Ordinance, any existing applicable laws and their corresponding Implementing Rules
and Regulations, Executive Orders, and other issuances relative thereto shall apply in a supplemental manner.

## Section 14.

This Ordinance shall take effect upon its posting in the City's official website, in the bulletin boards at the
entrance of the City Hall and SP Session Hall, and in at least two (2) conspicuous places in the City.

ENACTED ON 05 FEBRUARY 2024.
