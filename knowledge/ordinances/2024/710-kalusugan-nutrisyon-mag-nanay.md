# ORDINANCE NO. 19-2024

AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING AND IMPLEMENTING REPUBLIC ACT NO. 11148, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE *KALUSUGAN AT NUTRISYON NG
MAG-NANAY ACT*, AND PRESIDENTIAL DECREE NO. 1569 OR THE BARANGAY NUTRITION SCHOLAR LAW

Introduced by: Hon. Bernie A. Ortiz and Hon. Rebecca L. Reyes
*Committee on Health*

## EXPLANATORY NOTE

*WHEREAS*, Section 15, Article II of the 1987 Philippine Constitution emphasizes that "The State shall protect and
promote the right to health of the people and instill health consciousness among them";

*WHEREAS*, Republic Act (RA) No. 11148, otherwise known as the "*Kalusugan at Nutrisyon ng Mag-Nanay Act*", mandates
the scaling up of the national and local health and nutrition programs through a strengthened integrated strategy for
maternal, neonatal, child health and nutrition in the first one thousand (1,000) days of life;

*WHEREAS*, Presidential Decree (PD) No. 1569 mandates local government units (LGUs) to strengthen their Barangay
Nutrition Programs by providing for a barangay nutrition scholar in every barangay;

*WHEREAS*, the magnitude of the malnutrition problem in the country urgently needs greater involvement and
participation of the people at the grassroots level;

*WHEREAS*, this Body is cognizant that a selected individual from within the barangay who will be intensively trained
would be in a better position to deliver quality nutrition and health services particularly to target critical
families;

*WHEREAS*, in compliance with existing laws, rules and regulations, as well as the Seal of Good Local Governance
requirements, particularly on Health Compliance and Responsiveness, to improve and ensure that local health services
meet established standards;

*NOW, THEREFORE*, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE SANGGUNIANG PANLUNGSOD IN REGULAR SESSION ASSEMBLED THAT:

<!-- page 2 -->

## SECTION 1. *Short Title*

This Ordinance shall be known as the "ADOPTION OF KALUSUGAN AT NUTRISYON NG MAG-NANAY ACT (RA 11148) AND BARANGAY
NUTRITION SCHOLAR LAW (PD 1569) ORDINANCE" of the City of Carmona, Province of Cavite.

## SECTION 2. *Adoption*

The following national laws on health are hereby adopted, to wit:

- a. RA 11148 - Kalusugan at Nutrisyon ng Mag-Nanay Act; and
- b. PD No. 1569 - Barangay Nutrition Scholar Law.

## SECTION 3. *Local Action Nutrition Plan*

The Office of the City Health Officer (OCHO), in coordination with the Department of Health, National Nutrition
Council, Department of Agriculture, other national government agencies, civil society organizations, and other
stakeholders, shall develop a comprehensive and sustainable strategy for the first one thousand (1,000) days of life to
address the health, nutrition, and developmental problems affecting infants, young children, adolescent females, and
pregnant and lactating women. It shall operationalize the latest national and city plans on nutrition, integrating the
short, medium, and long-term plans of the government in response to the global call to eradicate hunger, improve
nutrition, and prevent and manage malnutrition, as one (1) of the seventeen (17) Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

## SECTION 4. *Barangay Nutrition Scholar*

There shall be a barangay nutrition scholar (BNS) or designated barangay health worker (BHW) in every barangay who
shall be responsible for delivering nutrition services and other related activities such as community health,
backyard food production, environmental sanitation, culture, mental feeding, and family planning to the barangay.

## SECTION 5. *Implementing Mechanisms*

The OCHO and the City Nutrition Committee are hereby tasked to effectively implement the foregoing national laws at
the barangay level through the barangay health centers, in coordination with the Liga ng mga Barangay. The BNS and the
BHWs shall be mobilized and provided with resources and benefits to carry out their tasks. In addition thereto, the
OCHO shall integrate maternal, neonatal, child health and nutrition programs in the local nutrition action plans and
investment plans for health.

## Section 6.

Let copy of this Ordinance be furnished the offices of the Sangguniang Panlalawigan, City Mayor, City Health Officer,
and other concerned offices for their information and appropriate action.

## Section 7.

This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon its approval.

ENACTED ON 2 DECEMBER 2024.
