# MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO. 07-2023

AN ORDINANCE PROVIDING FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT, MAINTENANCE, AND REGULATION OF PUBLIC OPEN PARKS AND GREEN SPACES IN
THE MUNICIPALITY OF CARMONA, CAVITE, PROVIDING PENALTIES FOR VIOLATION THEREOF, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Introduced by: Hon. Bernie A. Ortiz, Hon. Roel Z. Manarin, Hon. Domingo R. Tenedero, and ABC President, Hon. Reynaldo
K. Reyes
*Committee on Environmental Protection*

## EXPLANATORY NOTE

*WHEREAS*, Section 16, Article II of the 1987 Philippine Constitution provides that the State shall protect and
advance the right of the people to a balanced and healthful ecology in accord with the rhythm and harmony of nature;

*WHEREAS*, Sections 17 and 447 of Republic Act (RA) No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991,
mandate municipalities to establish tree parks, greenbelts, public spaces, and similar forest development projects;

*WHEREAS*, the Philippine Development Plan 2023-2028 also calls for an increase in the area of green spaces as a
prerequisite in creating livable communities;

<!-- page 2 -->

*WHEREAS*, the protection of the environment is vital since it sustains our life and existence;

*WHEREAS*, after a series of deliberations on the matter and due consultations with the concerned, this Body finds the
same in order and appropriate meriting its approval;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE SANGUNIANG BAYAN IN REGULAR SESSION ASSEMBLED THAT:

## SECTION 1. TITLE

This Ordinance shall be known and cited as the "Public Open Parks and Green Spaces (POPGS) Ordinance" of the
Municipality of Carmona.

## SECTION 2. PURPOSE

This ordinance is enacted to protect and advance the right of the people to a balanced and healthful ecology in accord
with the rhythm and harmony of nature particularly the establishment of public open parks and green spaces.

## SECTION 3. DECLARATION OF PUBLIC OPEN PARKS AND GREEN SPACES

The following are hereby declared as Public Open Parks and Green Spaces:

| Name | Area (sqm) | General Land Use | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Hon. Marciano Mapanoo Park | 2,552.10 | Open Park, Covered Basketball Court with Stage | Poblacion 4 (Town Plaza) |
| 2. Carmona Community Center (C3) Park | 13,402.56 | Open Park, Playground and Multi-purpose Facility | Brgy. Cabilang Baybay |
| 3. Carmona Municipal Park | 13,742.26 | Open Park | Brgy. Lantic |

## SECTION 4. ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION SCHEMES

The Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office (MENRO) and the Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO), in close
collaboration with the concerned stakeholders, are hereby urged to develop, implement, maintain, and showcase
landscaping, Tree Protection, Urban Greening, Arbor Day, and Heritage Tree Programs and other environmental protection
measures such as pollution reduction, resource exploitation, inventory of trees, monitoring and maintenance of public
open parks and green spaces, and the likes. The MENRO and the Municipal Information, Tourism, Culture and Arts Office
(MITCAO) shall also conduct environmental protection awareness campaign to educate the constituents on how to protect
our environment.

<!-- page 3 -->

## SECTION 5. FREE USE OF PUBLIC OPEN PARKS AND GREEN SPACES

The use of the abovementioned POPGS, except for the C3 Multi-purpose Facility, shall be free of charge and for public
use. The same shall not be used nor occupied for any purpose whatsoever, other than for ornament and recreation,
government functions, holding or conduct of meetings, games, sports, healthy lifestyle, or physical exercises, provided
that a permit for the holding or conduct of said activities is first secured from the Office of the Mayor.

## SECTION 6. PROHIBITIONS

Unless otherwise provided in this Ordinance or its Supplemental Guidelines, it shall be unlawful for any person to:

- a. Cut, chop, or destroy plants, trees, or any ornaments used for landscaping, greening, and beautification of the
  POPGS or to commit any form of vandalism or posting of unauthorized advertisement or signage that tends to destroy
  their original setting or aesthetic features;
- b. Establish, operate, and/or maintain a sari-sari store, eateries, cafeterias, bars, *tiangge*, or any structure or
  booth within the POPGS; to sell or expose for sale beers, liquors or any goods, wares or merchandise of any
  description, are prohibited, except those used for exhibits of local products or promotion/selling of local farm
  harvests duly approved by the Office of the Mayor;
- c. Erect, post, fence, stand, or stage any structures or objects abutting the monument in the Hon. Marciano Mapanoo
  Park, or to place, nail or tie posters, signboards, streamers, or other ads or decorations on the said monument;
- d. Litter, dump, or burn solid wastes; and
- e. Exhaust resources such as electricity and water without the approval of the local government.

## SECTION 7. PENAL PROVISIONS

- a. Any person who violates this Ordinance shall shall be given a citation ticket for every offense and be imposed
  with the following penalties:

| Offense | Penalty |
|---|---|
| a. First Offense | A fine of One Thousand Pesos (PhP 1,000.00) |
| b. Second Offense | A fine of Two Thousand Pesos (PhP 2,000.00) |
| c. Third and Succeeding Offense | A fine of Two Thousand Five Hundred Pesos (PhP 2,500.00) and/or imprisonment of not exceeding one (1) month at the sound discretion of the competent court. |

*(Note: For minor offenders, penalties governing minors shall apply)*

<!-- page 4 -->

- b. The offender shall pay the corresponding fine/s at the Municipal Treasurer's Office within five (5) working days
  from the issuance of the citation ticket.
- c. Failure to pay the fine herein imposed within the said period shall entail that the offender shall be
  disqualified from receiving assistance and other benefits from the Barangay and Municipal Governments including the
  issuance of barangay or police clearance for a period of six (6) months reckoning from the time when such violation
  was committed and until the said fine is paid. In addition thereto, after the lapse of two (2) months, the Municipal
  Legal Office may file an appropriate legal action against the offender of this Ordinance.

## SECTION 8. IMPLEMENTING MECHANISMS

The MENRO, Carmona Municipal Police Station (CMPS), and barangay officials/tanods, shall be responsible for the
effective implementation of this Ordinance. The Municipal Mayor may issue supplemental guidelines of this Ordinance, if
deemed necessary.

## Section 9. REPEALING CLAUSE

All municipal ordinances, implementing rules and regulations, and executive orders, or parts thereof, which are
inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed and/or modified accordingly.

## Section 10. SEPARATIVITY CLAUSE

If any provision or portion of this ordinance is held unconstitutional or invalid, other provisions or portions hereof
which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

## Section 11. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE.

This Ordinance shall take effect upon compliance of the mandatory posting requirement prescribed under RA 7160.
