# MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO. 02-2022

AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING THE REVISED DRUG PRICE REFERENCE INDEX OF THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH (DOH) PURSUANT TO REPUBLIC
ACT NO. 9502, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE "UNIVERSALLY ACCESSIBLE AND QUALITY MEDICINES ACT OF 2008", THEREBY AMENDING
SECTION 4D.01, ARTICLE D OF THE REVISED REVENUE CODE OF 2017 (MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO. 013-2016)

*Introduced by: Hon. Roel Z. Manarin and Hon. Cesar M. Loyola*
*Joint Committees on Ways & Means and Health, Energy & Environmental Protection*

## EXPLANATORY NOTE

WHEREAS, Republic Act (RA) 9502 or the "Universally Accessible Cheaper and Quality Medicines Act of 2008" accords the
DOH the power to monitor and regulate the prices of drugs and medicines to protect consumers and the public procuring
entities from excessive mark-ups applied to medicines resulting from information asymmetry and the lack of effective
market competition;

WHEREAS, the DOH Pharmaceutical Division established the Drug Price Reference Index (DPRI), a national drug pricing
strategy which aims to guide all national and local government health facilities in the efficient sourcing of
pharmaceutical products in the public sector;

WHEREAS, the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) issued Memorandum Circular No. 2017-06 advising
LGUs to procure medicines at the same price or at lower than the DPRI;

<!-- page 2 -->

WHEREAS, the DOH, through Administrative Order No. 2019-0040, issued guidelines on the implementation of the DPRI to
all public hospitals and health facilities and mandated the use of DPRI as the ceiling price for drug procurement;

WHEREAS, the fees imposed under Section 4D.01, Article D of the Revised Revenue Code of 2017 for the health/medical
services/equipment/supplies being provided for by the Municipal Health Office (MHO) and Pagamutang Bayan ng Carmona
(PBC) do not conform with the revised DPRI;

WHEREAS, there is a need to amend the fees imposed under the above provision of the ordinance in order to coincide
with the existing laws, issuances, rules and regulations with the view of promoting and ensuring access to quality and
affordable medicines and medical services for all;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE SANGGUNIANG BAYAN IN REGULAR SESSION ASSEMBLED THAT:

## Section 1.

The Revised DPRI of the DOH is hereby adopted thereby amending Section 4D.01, Article D of Municipal Ordinance No.
13-2016 or the Revised Revenue Code of 2017.

## Section 2.

The List of Medicines and Medical Supplies/Services with corresponding adjusted fees submitted by the Head of Hospital
of the Pagamutang Bayan ng Carmona (PBC) and attached herewith as "Annex A" is hereby approved and shall be made an
integral part of this Ordinance.

[annex not in this file: Annex A, List of Medicines and Medical Supplies/Services with corresponding adjusted fees]

## Section 3.

The Municipal Health Office and PBC are hereby tasked to effectively implement this Ordinance.

## Section 4.

Let copy of this Ordinance be furnished the offices of the Sangguniang Panlalawigan, Municipal Mayor, MHO, PBC and
other concerned offices for their information and appropriate action.

## Section 5.

This Ordinance shall be in full force and effect after its publication and/or posting on the Bulletin Board of the
Municipal Hall and other conspicuous places in the Municipality.

ENACTED ON 28 FEBRUARY 2022.
