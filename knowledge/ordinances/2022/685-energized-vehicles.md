# MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO. 16-2022

AN ORDINANCE REGULATING THE OPERATION OF ENERGIZED VEHICLES (E-VEHICLES) WITHIN THE TERRITORIAL JURISDICTION OF THE
MUNICIPALITY OF CARMONA, CAVITE, PROVIDING PENALTIES FOR VIOLATION THEREOF, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Introduced by: Hon. Maximo A. Acbay and Hon. Bernie A. Ortiz
*Committee on Transportation*

## EXPLANATORY NOTE

*WHEREAS*, Sections 16 and 447 (a)(5)(vi) of the Local Government Code of 1991 (RA 7160) provide the power of
municipalities to regulate traffic in all streets and bridges within their territorial boundaries, and to enact
measures that will enhance public health, safety, convenience, maintain peace and order, and promote the general
welfare of their inhabitants;

*WHEREAS*, data shows that there is an alarming increase of e-vehicles in Carmona which serve as private carriers or
school services, some of which are driven by minor individuals/students;

*WHEREAS*, the unregulated use and operation of e-bikes and e-trikes on highways and other intense major traffic routes
expose their owners and drivers, as well as their passengers, especially minors/children, to the risk of serious
bodily harm or fatality, including liability for damages;

<!-- page 2 -->

*WHEREAS*, while the Municipal Government of Carmona promotes these light electric vehicles for being economical,
environment-friendly, noise-free, and convenient, it also recognizes the necessity of this Ordinance in safeguarding
life and property of its constituents especially children;

*WHEREAS*, in view of the abovementioned mandates, this Body conducted series of consultations with the Land
Transportation Office - Carmona, concerned municipal offices, barangay chairpersons and concerned committees, and other
stakeholders, including a public hearing conducted on 21 October 2022 where majority of the participants signified
their consensus on the issue at hand;

*NOW, THEREFORE*, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE SANGGUNIANG BAYAN IN REGULAR SESSION ASSEMBLED THAT:

## Section 1. Short Title.

This Ordinance shall be known and cited as the "E-Vehicle Ordinance of Carmona, Cavite".

## Section 2. Purposes.

This Ordinance is hereby enacted to regulate the operation of e-vehicles within the territorial jurisdiction of the
Municipality of Carmona, and to promote the general welfare, including among others, to promote health and safety,
enhance the right of the people to a balanced ecology, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and
convenience of the constituents.

## Section 3. Coverage.

This Ordinance shall apply to all types of e-bikes and e-trikes driven within, or passing through the Municipality of
Carmona, and the drivers and owners thereof.

## Section 4. Interpretation.

Words and phrases not defined in this Ordinance shall be given their plain or customary meanings, unless the context
requires otherwise. All words, phrases, and provisions shall be interpreted in a manner that is consistent with the
purpose and spirit of this Ordinance.

## Section 5. Definition of Terms.

For purposes of this Ordinance, the following terms shall mean:

- a) Barangay roads - refer to all streets/roads within the jurisdiction of the Municipal Government of Carmona, not
  otherwise designated as national, provincial and municipal roads.

<!-- page 3 -->

- b) Electric bicycle or e-bike - a motor vehicle powered by electrical energy from rechargeable batteries which are
  designed to travel on two, three or four wheels for two passengers including the driver thereof.
- c) Electric tricycle or e-trike - a motor vehicle powered by electrical energy from rechargeable batteries
  symmetrically arranged mounted with cabin which is designed to travel on three wheels, for six (6) or less
  passengers excluding the driver depending on the number seats provision. (*LTO MC No. AVT-2015-1983*)
- d) Energized Vehicle or E-Vehicle - refers to e-bike and e-trike.
- e) E-Vehicle Permit to Operate - a permit allowing the use and operation of an e-vehicle within Carmona.
- f) E-Vehicle Driver's Permit (EVDP) - a permit authorizing a person to use and operate an e-vehicle.
- g) High-end e-bikes - defined as high powered and high speed e-bikes with 800 watts and above. E-bikes under this
  classification have the ability to navigate safely on main roads through a fast moving traffic. Furthermore, these
  models are no different in terms of performance (e.g. speed) to average gasoline type motorcycle.
- h) Low-end e-bikes - defined as low powered or low speed e-bikes with 650 watts and below. E-bikes under this
  classification have difficulty navigating through a fast moving traffic.
- i) Municipal roads - refer to roads within the Poblacion; roads that connect to provincial and national roads; roads
  that provide inter-barangay connections to major municipal infrastructure without traversing provincial roads.

## Section 6. Mandatory Registration of E-Vehicles for Private Use.

All e-vehicles for private use shall be registered with the Municipal Tricycle Franchising and Regulatory Board
(MTFRB) upon payment of fees stated under Section 8 hereof and prior to the issuance of an EVDP. The registration of
e-vehicles shall be valid for three (3) years and renewable thereafter.

Late registration of e-vehicles shall subject their registered owners to a surcharge of Twenty-Five percent (25%) of
the total amount of the applicable fees for renewal in addition to the penalty being imposed under Section 17 hereof.

<!-- page 4 -->

Non-Carmona residents, when driving e-vehicles and passing through Carmona, shall be required to present official
receipts, certificate of ownership, or notarized Deed of Absolute Sale/Mortgage Agreement and LTO driver's license to
the traffic enforcer. Registration of e-vehicles owned by non-Carmona residents is not mandated under this Ordinance.

Any documents or official receipts in case of delivering new e-vehicles shall be presented to the proper authority.

## Section 7. Documentary Requirements.

The following documentary requirements for the registration of e-vehicles shall be submitted to the MTFRB:

- a. E-vehicle Registration Form;
- b. Official Receipt (OR) and Deed of Sale or Mortgage Agreement (notarial documents may be accepted in lieu
  thereof);
- c. Community Tax Certificate (CTC) for the current year;
- d. Barangay Clearance;
- e. 2x2 ID picture of the applicant; and
- f. One (1) valid ID preferably PSA-issued National ID, COMELEC/Voter's ID, or any other Philippine
  government-issued ID indicating the residency and date of birth.

After registration, the MTFRB shall issue an E-Vehicle Permit to Operate together with certificate of registration to
the applicant as proof of compliance.

## Section 8. Regulatory Fees.

The following fees shall be paid to the Municipal Treasurer's Office:

- a. E-Vehicle Tin Plate - PhP200.00 (valid for 6 years)
- b. Regulation Sticker - PhP 50.00 (valid for 3 years)
- c. EVDP ID Card - PhP 50.00 (valid for 3 years)
- d. E-vehicle Permit - PhP200.00 (valid for 3 years)
- e. Certificates of Registration and Attendance/Completion : FREE

## Section 9. E-Vehicle License Plate and Sticker.

After registration and payment of appropriate fees, a license plate shall be issued to the applicant which shall be
permanently attached at the back portion of the registered e-vehicle. The e-vehicle sticker shall be issued and posted
by MTFRB personnel in the front portion of the e-vehicle. The e-vehicle tin plates and stickers shall be color-coded
to easily identify as to whether an e-vehicle is for hire, personal, or business use. The allowed number of passengers
and maximum weight capacity shall be indicated on the sticker.

<!-- page 5 -->

## Section 10. E-Vehicle Driver's Permit.

In the absence of driver's license issued by the Land Transportation Office (LTO), e-vehicle drivers shall secure an
E-Vehicle Driver's Permit (EVDP) with MTFRB who shall have the following requirements:

- a.) Must be a bonafide resident of Carmona;
- b.) At least eighteen (18) years old;
- c.) Must secure a Barangay Clearance in the place where he/she resides or submit photocopy of his/her Registry of
  Barangay Inhabitants (RBI) ID, if applicable;
- d.) Must submit one (1) valid ID preferably PSA-issued National ID, COMELEC/Voter's ID, or any other Philippine
  government-issued ID indicating the residency and date of birth;
- e.) Certificate of Attendance to E-Vehicle Driver's Professionalization Program (E-VDPP) orientation-seminar; and
- f.) Certificate of Completion for passing the appropriate written and practical examinations.

The EVDP shall be valid for three (3) years and must be renewed thereafter on or before the birthday of the applicant
upon showing of previous Permit and payment of renewal fee in the amount of Two Hundred Pesos (PhP200.00).

## Section 11. E-Vehicle Driver's Professionalization Program.

Applicants of E-Vehicle Driver's Permit shall undergo E-Vehicle Driver's Professionalization Program (E-VDPP)
orientation-seminar which shall include among others, basic traffic regulations, road courtesy, traffic offenses,
pertinent provisions of the Carmona Traffic Management Code, and the provisions of this Ordinance that are relevant to
their operation.

Participants to the E-VDPP orientation shall be issued a Certificate of Attendance and a Certificate of Completion for
passing the written and practical examinations which shall be presented to the MTFRB before releasing the EVDP.

The MTFRB shall draft the syllabus of the orientation-seminar, written examination, and the conduct of the actual
training.

## Section 12. Specifications/Physical Requirements.

No e-vehicles shall be registered unless the following specifications or physical requirements are complied with and
certified by MTFRB:

- a.) Side Mirrors - Side mirrors should be installed at the front of the driver seat to monitor other vehicles
  travelling at the back.

<!-- page 6 -->

- b.) Back and Interior Light - Back light should be opened while travelling, traversing, and executing a left or right
  turn. Interior light in the form of luminous bulbs not less than twenty one (21) candle light power shall be set from
  sundown to sunset while driving the vehicle;
- c.) Clean windshields (if applicable); and
- d.) Road worthiness of the unit.

## Section 13. Prohibited Acts.

This Ordinance prohibits the following acts:

- a.) Driving an unregistered or expired registration of e-vehicle, or without an E-Vehicle Driver's Permit and/or
  LTO-issued Driver's License;
- b.) Driving low-end or high-end e-bike without wearing a standard safety helmet securely fastened under the chin, and
  other safety gears such as shoes, pants, and motorcycle jacket;
- c.) Driving a low-end e-vehicle along Governor's Drive;
- d.) Not using the outermost lane/outer edge of a road or e-vehicle lane when driving an e-vehicle on other secondary
  roads, except on barangay roads;
- e.) No e-vehicle shall carry at one time more persons/goods than what is allowed (overloading);
- f.) Non-compliance with specifications or physical requirements of an e-vehicle;
- g.) Driving a private e-vehicle for a fee; and
- h.) Minors driving e-vehicles.

## Section 14. Other Restrictions.

High-end e-vehicles may be allowed to be driven along Governor's Drive and other major/minor roads and thoroughfares in
the Municipality. While low-end e-vehicles may only be allowed to use major roads during emergency cases or unusual
occurrence of events or force majeure.

The use of low-end e-vehicles with 650 watts or below shall be limited within or along municipal and barangay roads
only. It may pass the main thoroughfares, highways, or national road for purposes of crossing only when the municipal
or barangay road is divided by a highway, main thoroughfares, or the national road.

<!-- page 7 -->

Further, drivers of high-end e-bikes with 800 watts or above shall possess LTO-issued driver's license and/or EVDP
when travelling along Governor's Drive.

## Section 15. E-Trikes for Hire.

E-trikes for hire shall be governed by the rules and regulations as provided for by the Revised Traffic Management
Code of Carmona.

## Section 16. Implementing Mechanisms.

The MTFRB, CARTMO, and all barangay chairpersons/tanods, in close coordination with the Carmona Municipal Police
Station, Municipal Treasurer's Office, and Municipal Information Technology Unit, are hereby tasked and authorized to
enforce the provisions of this Ordinance.

MTFRB shall maintain a record of all e-vehicles based on the applications or plate numbers and stickers issued through
digitalization, shall monitor and update violations through local interconnectivity with the CMPS, MTO, CARTMO, and
other concerned offices, shall include and prominently display the process in its Citizens' Charter, and shall prepare
all the documentary forms needed for the effective implementation of this Ordinance.

However, the Office of the Mayor may issue supplemental rules and regulations for proper implementation of this
Ordinance.

## Section 17. Penal Provisions.

a. Any person who violates any provisions of this Ordinance shall be meted with the following fines and penalties:

| Violation | First Offense | Second Offense | 3rd/Succeeding Offense |
|---|---|---|---|
| i. Unregistered e-vehicle - driving without license/permit - without license plate/sticker | PhP 1,000.00 | PhP1,500.00 | PhP2,500.00 plus impoundment |
| ii. Not wearing standard safety helmet | PhP 500.00 | PhP1,000.00 | PhP1,500.00 plus seminar |
| iii. Driving/plying on a prohibited road | PhP 500.00 | PhP1,000.00 | PhP1,500.00 plus seminar |
| iv. Not using the e-bike lane or outermost lane | PhP 500.00 | PhP1,000.00 | PhP1,500.00 plus seminar |

<!-- page 8 -->

| Violation | First Offense | Second Offense | 3rd/Succeeding Offense |
|---|---|---|---|
| v. Overloading | PhP 500.00 | PhP1,000.00 | PhP1,500.00 plus seminar |
| vi. Non-compliance with specifications or physical requirements | PhP 500.00 | PhP1,000.00 | PhP1,500.00 plus suspension of permit to use/operate |
| vii. Driving a private e-vehicle for a fee | PhP 500.00 | PhP1,000.00 | PhP1,500.00 plus suspension of permit to use/operate |
| vii. Minor offender | PhP 500.00 plus impoundment | PhP1,000.00 plus impoundment | PhP1,500.00 plus impoundment |

- b. Where the violator is a driver, the EVDP shall be confiscated and an Ordinance Violation Receipt (OVR) or Traffic
  Citation Ticket (TCT) shall be issued to the offender by the apprehending traffic enforcer or CMPS personnel.
- c. The violator shall have five (5) days from date of violation within which to redeem his/her license/permit which
  shall only be released upon presentation of OVR/TCT and proof of payment of the appropriate fines. An additional
  surcharge of five percent (5%) for each day of delay or failure to pay the appropriate fine is hereby imposed until
  the full amount of fine and surcharge is paid.
- d. In the event that the apprehended driver has no driver's license or EVDP, the e-vehicle is unregistered/late
  registered, or the offender is a minor, the e-vehicle shall be impounded at the CARTMO's designated impounding area.
  The same shall only be released until the provisions of this Ordinance have been complied with and upon payment of
  the fines imposed under this Section, towing fee amounting to PhP300 for e-bikes/PhP500 for e-trikes, and the
  impounding fee amounting to PhP50.00 per day of impoundment.
- e. If a violator or parent/legal custodian of a minor offender is unable to pay the fines imposed, he or she may
  choose to render community service to the municipality. For every hour of community service rendered, his
  outstanding fine shall be reduced by an amount equivalent to triple the hourly minimum wage of the municipality.

## Section 18. Supplementary Clause.

On matters not provided in this Ordinance, pertinent provisions of the Carmona Revised Traffic Management Code or any
existing applicable laws and their corresponding implementing rules and regulations, executive orders and relevant
issuances therefor shall be applied in a supplemental manner.

<!-- page 9 -->

## Section 19. Repealing Clause.

All municipal ordinances, implementing rules and regulations, and executive orders, or parts thereof, which are
inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed and/or modified accordingly.

## Section 20. Separability Clause.

If, for any reason or reasons, any part or provision of this Ordinance shall be held unconstitutional or invalid, other
parts or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

## Section 21. Effectivity Clause.

This Ordinance shall take effect upon compliance with the mandatory posting and/or publication requirement prescribed
under R.A. 7160.
