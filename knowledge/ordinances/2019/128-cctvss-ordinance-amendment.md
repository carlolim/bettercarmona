# MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO. 15-2019

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTIONS 3, 4, 10, AND 11 OF MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO. 013-2013, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE
"CLOSED-CIRCUIT TELEVISION SURVEILLANCE SYSTEM (CCTVSS) ORDINANCE OF CARMONA, CAVITE"

Introduced by: Hon. Cesar M. Loyola and Hon. Huberto A. Hugo

*NOW, THEREFORE,* BE IT ORDAINED BY THE SANGGUNIANG BAYAN IN REGULAR SESSION ASSEMBLED THAT:

## Section 1.

Section 3 of Municipal Ordinance No. 013-2013, otherwise known as the "CLOSED-CIRCUIT TELEVISION SURVEILLANCE SYSTEM
(CCTVSS) Ordinance of Carmona, Cavite", is hereby amended to read as follows:

"Section 3. COVERAGE. -

xxx

(b) The environmental enforcement of this Ordinance shall apply to all pollution control facilities such as air
pollution control installations, hazardous waste facilities, solid waste facilities, and wastewater facilities."

## Section 2.

Section 4 of the same Ordinance is hereby further amended to read as follows:

<!-- page 2 -->

"Section 4. MINIMUM TECHNOLOGICAL STANDARDS.-

xxx

For this purpose, it shall meet the following minimum specifications:

- a. Have one dedicated channel for each camera in operation;
- b. Shall record at least 1080p recording resolution level;
- c. Shall have enough memory to retain data from all cameras for a period of sixty (60) days.
- d. Possess the ability to view and retrieve data while the system remains in operation.
- e. Possess the ability to produce full HD copy of desired data for evidentiary purposes on a format playable via
  Windows Player.
- f. Possess the ability to time stamp and "watermark" the recorded images.
- g. Shall be placed in a locked and secured location to prevent destruction or tampering.

Cameras

- h. Operate with a minimum of two (2) megapixel for IP camera.
- i. Shall have the ability to record color images during sufficient lighting and record in black and white during
  hours of low light.
- j. Each camera shall be matched to each specific application taking into consideration:
  - i. the distance to a target image;
  - ii. "Lux rating" or compatibility with the amount of light available to include excessive sunlight; and
  - iii. View angle of camera in relation to an area of a desired coverage.
- k. Each camera shall have a clear and unobstructed view of the area of desired coverage.
- l. Cameras shall be positioned to capture "Head and Shoulder" images at exits, entrances, and points of sale
  location.
- m. The CCTV cameras installed shall produce a recognizable, retrievable, enlargeable, and reproducible photographic
  images of persons and that they shall be of sufficient clarity for identification purposes.

<!-- page 3 -->

Network Video Recorder (NVR) Monitor

- n. Each system shall have a monitor that may be assessed by the CMPS and other law enforcement agencies for viewing
  of the recorded images.
- o. The monitor shall be of a Liquid Crystal Display (LCD) design with a screen no smaller than fifteen (15) diagonal
  inches.

Power Supply

Each system shall have a dedicated power source to prevent intentional or accidental deactivation and preferably
provided with Uninterrupted Power Supply (UPS).

Environmental Enforcement

In addition to the abovementioned requirements and for the purpose of environmental enforcement mentioned in Section
3 hereof, the following minimum specifications shall be required:

- a. Minimum of four (4) bullet two (2) megapixel IP cameras with Dark Fighter (or any feature that will make video
  recording color); and
- b. NVR with P2P.

## Section 3.

Section 10 thereof is likewise hereby amended to replace the Municipal Information Technology Officer by the Building
Official as chairperson of the Municipal CCTVSS Enforcement Team (MCET).

## Section 4.

Section 11 of the same Ordinance is hereby further amended to read as follows:

"Section 11. ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS. -

xxx

The use of drones by concerned municipal departments, their inspectors, MCET, and other government instrumentalities
in the course of their official functions, is hereby authorized.

<!-- page 4 -->

## Section 5.

All municipal ordinances, implementing rules and regulations, and executive orders, or parts thereof, which are
inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed and/or modified accordingly.

## Section 6.

This Ordinance shall take effect upon compliance of the mandatory posting requirement prescribed under RA 7160,
otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991.
