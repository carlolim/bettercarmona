    6. Pagsusunog para sa pagsasanay o pag-aaral [1].
    7. Pagsusunog na kilalang praktika sa pangangasiwa ng pansakahan, kabundukan o kagubatan [1].
    8. Pagsusunog na may pahintulot mula sa Kagawaran ng Kapaligiran at Likas Yaman [1].

Expected: Suggested on 2026-11-14 and approved by the user. A sensitive question about a service on the site: leads with timely death registration and gives its requirements, starting with the Certificate of Death.

    To register the death of a person who died in the City of Carmona within thirty (30) days of the death, you must go to the Local Civil Registry Office [1]. 
    
    Requirements include:
    1. One (1) set of Certificate of Death from a hospital or funeral parlor [1].
    2. Autopsy Report or Police Report, if applicable [1].
    3. Certification of Embalmer from the funeral services [1].
    
    Steps:
    1. Submit the accomplished Certificate of Death to the office [1].
    2. Pay at the Treasury Office. Fees are Php 50.00 for a burial permit or Php 100.00 for a transfer of cadaver, according to the Citizen's Charter 2026 [1].
    3. Return to the Local Civil Registry Office to receive the registered document [1].
