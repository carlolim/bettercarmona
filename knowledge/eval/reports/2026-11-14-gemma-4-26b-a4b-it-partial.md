> Is there e-bike regulation in Carmona?

Expected: The user's question, 2026-11-14. Municipal Ordinance No. 16-2022 (library item 685) regulates energized vehicles, including e-bikes; transcribed the same day.

    Yes, there is an ordinance regulating the operation of Energized Vehicles (E-Vehicles), which includes e-bikes and e-trikes, within the Municipality of Carmona [1]. Under Municipal Ordinance No. 16-2022, enacted on November 7, 2022, and approved on November 8, 2022, the following rules apply:
    
    1. Private e-vehicles must be registered with the Municipal Tricycle Franchising and Regulatory Board (MTFRB) [1].
    2. Registration is valid for three years [1].
    3. Drivers without an LTO license must obtain an E-Vehicle Driver's Permit (EVDP) and must be at least 18 years old [1].
    4. Certain acts are prohibited, such as minors driving e-vehicles, riding without a standard safety helmet, overloading, and driving low-end e-bikes (650 watts and below) along Governor's Drive [1].
